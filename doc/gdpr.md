# GDPR Compliance Statement

**Last Updated:** _2026-01-26_

TeamsVote is designed with privacy in mind and operates in accordance with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679. This section explains how TeamsVote aligns with GDPR requirements.

---

## Lawful Basis for Processing

TeamsVote does not create its own user accounts and does not store personal data. Any user identity used during a session is provided by Microsoft Teams and processed for the legitimate interest of enabling the voting functionality within the Teams environment.

---

## Data Minimization

In accordance with Article 5(1)(c), TeamsVote follows strict data minimization practices:

- No persistent personal data storage
- No replication of Microsoft account data
- No tracking, profiling, or analytics
- No export of personal data outside Microsoft Teams

---

## Data Storage & Retention

TeamsVote does **not** store or retain personal data. Session-related information is processed **in-memory only** and discarded automatically once the session ends. No logs or audit trails containing personal information are created.

---

## Data Transfers

TeamsVote does not transfer data to third countries or external processors. All relevant data remains within the Microsoft Teams infrastructure as governed by your organization’s Microsoft 365 tenant policies.

---

## Data Subject Rights

Because TeamsVote does not store or persist personal data, data subject rights such as:

- access
- rectification
- erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
- portability
- objection
- restriction of processing

do not generally apply to TeamsVote directly. Data subject queries should be directed to the user’s Microsoft 365 administrator or organization, which controls identity and data access.

---

## Data Controller & Data Processor Status

For the purposes of GDPR:

- Microsoft acts as the **data processor** for identity and access management within Teams.
- The user’s organization acts as the **data controller** for all account-related personal data.
- TeamsVote does **not** act as a controller or processor for stored personal data, as no persistent data processing occurs.

---

## Security and Technical Measures

TeamsVote inherits security controls from Microsoft Teams and complies with Article 32 by ensuring that no personal data is stored, moved, or transmitted beyond the Teams environment.
